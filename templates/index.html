About This System

This application demonstrates real-time facial analysis using OpenCV and deep learning models.

It can detect faces, predict age and gender, and recognize individuals that have been trained into its database.

How It Works

  • Click "Start Recognition" to activate your webcam and begin analysis.
  • Faces detected will be highlighted, and their attributes (name, age, gender) displayed below.
  • "Unknown" appears for faces not in the trained database.
  • Use "Toggle Dark Mode" to switch between light and dark themes.

Ensure your camera is enabled for the best experience.
